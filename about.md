# About
{: #about-heading}

I am a Senior Director of Compiler Engineering at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com), where I lead a team working on compiler and agent co-design. Our goal is to shape what a compiler becomes once agents are first-class users of it.

Before NVIDIA, I was a Science Director and Principal Scientist at [Annapurna Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annapurna_Labs), Amazon, where I led a Machine Learning Systems (MLSys) science team working on [AWS Trainium](https://aws.amazon.com/machine-learning/trainium/), optimizing systems and compilers to train and serve large foundation models efficiently on homegrown accelerators. Earlier, I worked at the Parallel Computing Lab at [Intel Labs](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/overview.html). I received my Ph.D. in [Computer Science](https://www.cs.princeton.edu) and [Neuroscience](https://pni.princeton.edu) from [Princeton University](https://www.princeton.edu).

**We are hiring engineers and researchers at all levels.** If you are interested in compilers and agents in general, please don't hesitate to reach out to me.
